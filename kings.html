The King's Chamber

Granite walls, a silent sarcophagus, and four carved tablets. Someone broke the royal seal here. Compare the evidence, solve three puzzles, and earn two clues.

Puzzle 1 · The Four Tablets

Four tablets record the same night, but one scribe told a different story. Flip each tablet, compare the captions, then choose the odd one out.

Which tablet is different?

Yes! One scribe saw the door standing open.

Those captions match. Look for the one that says something different.

Puzzle 2 · The Scales of Maat

The left pan holds a weight of 9 (a 4 and a 5). Choose exactly two weights for the right pan so the scales balance.

Tap a weight again to remove it from the pan.

The scales settle level and the sarcophagus lid shifts.

The scales tip. Try a different pair.

Puzzle 3 · The Royal Offering

“Water cleanses before anything else. The bread is laid down before the incense is burned.” Give each offering its place in the ritual.

Water
Bread
Incense

The ritual is complete. A stone panel slides aside.

The ritual is wrong. The panel stays shut.

Chamber complete! The passage to the Queen's Chamber is open.

How to play

  1. Tap a tablet to flip it and read its caption.
  2. Compare the captions to spot the difference.
  3. Solve a puzzle to reveal a clue, then pick it up.
  4. Solve all three puzzles to unlock the next chamber.
Hint for Puzzle 1

Read every caption word by word. Only one word changes.

Hint for Puzzle 2

Two numbers that add up to nine. Not 4 and 5, because 5 is missing.

Hint for Puzzle 3

Water is first. Bread comes before incense.
