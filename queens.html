How to play

  1. Tap a drawer to slide it open and read what is inside.
  2. Put the papyri in date order and finish the pattern.
  3. Solve a puzzle to reveal a clue, then pick it up.
  4. Solve all three puzzles to open the final door.
Hint for Puzzle 1

Open all four drawers. Only one holds a key.

Hint for Puzzle 2

Smaller day numbers come first: 3, then 9, then 12.

Hint for Puzzle 3

Triangle, circle, square. Then the cycle starts again.
