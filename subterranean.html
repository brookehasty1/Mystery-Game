The Subterranean Chamber

You wake in the deepest chamber, carved into the rock beneath the pyramid. The golden scarab has been stolen, and the only way out is up. Solve the three puzzles and collect two clues.

Puzzle 1 · The Sun Door

“I climb the sky each dawn and vanish each dusk, yet I am no bird. Press my symbol.”

The door grinds open and a loose wall appears behind it.

Nothing happens. The door stays sealed.

Puzzle 2 · The Loose Stones

“A pyramid has four triangular faces. Push the stone with that number, then push the stone with half as many.”

Tap a stone again to pull it back out.

A hidden niche opens. Something is inside!

The wall shudders. A wrong stone is pushed in.

Puzzle 3 · The Sky Lock

Scratched above the lock: “First the giver of day, then the lady of the night, last the lights that never set.”

Dial 1
Dial 2
Dial 3

Click! The stone lock falls away.

The lock does not move. Re-read the riddle.

Chamber complete! The stairway to the King's Chamber is open.

How to play

  1. Read each riddle carefully.
  2. Click the symbols, stones, or dials to answer.
  3. Solve a puzzle to reveal a clue, then pick it up.
  4. Solve all three puzzles to unlock the next chamber.
Hint for Puzzle 1

Which one rises and sets every day?

Hint for Puzzle 2

Four faces, and half of four is two.

Hint for Puzzle 3

Day, night, then the lights in the night sky.
